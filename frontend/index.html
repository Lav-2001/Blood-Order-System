ABOUT BLOODCONNECT

Making Blood Access Simple & Faster.

BloodConnect is a blood order management platform designed to make it easier for people to find available blood and connect with blood banks.

Instead of visiting or calling different blood banks one by one, users can search blood banks, check blood availability and place a blood order through one platform.

  • Check blood availability easily
  • Find trusted blood banks
  • Place blood orders quickly
  • Track your order status

Our Mission

Our mission is to reduce the time people spend searching for blood by providing simple access to blood availability and blood bank information in one place.
